===========================================================
  n         H_n (Hanoi)      M_n (Mersenne)      H_n == M_n?
-----------------------------------------------------------
  1               1                   1              T
  2               3                   3              T
  3               7                   7              T
  4              15                  15              T

  6              63                  63              T

  8             255                 255              T

===========================================================
CONCLUSION: H_n = M_n for every n in [1, 10].
The minimal number of moves to solve the Tower of Hanoi
with n disks is always equal to the n-th Mersenne number.
